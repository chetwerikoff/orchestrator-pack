title: Rebase carry can use a stale narrower scope declaration
evidence: Condition 5 admits carry when the forward main-side paths do not match the active `orchestrator-pack/pr-scope-declaration/v1` `declared_paths` or `allowed_roots`. On current main, `PrScopeDeclaration.source_revision` is explicitly informational and “never used as a source or trust witness”; `pr-scope-check.ts` accepts a declaration when its `allowed_roots` are a subset of the live Issue `allowed-roots`, not necessarily equal to them. Therefore an Issue can widen its live allowed roots after a declaration was created while the older narrower declaration remains policy-compatible. A forward main-side change inside that newly added live root but outside the stale declaration's roots would be invisible to the bridge and could authorize carried PASS even though the change is inside the task's current declared scope.
recommendation: Make the bridge's overlap proof include the current trusted live Issue `allowed-roots` already obtained through trusted target resolution, or require an exact mechanically proven current-scope equivalence before a declaration can stand in for those roots. Add a regression where the live Issue widens scope after declaration creation and a forward main-side change in the newly added root must reject the bridge with `history_non_descendant`.
